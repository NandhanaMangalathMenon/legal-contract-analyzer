MASTER SOFTWARE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on 1 January 2026 between Alpha Tech Private Limited (the "Vendor") and Beta Retail Private Limited (the "Customer").

Page 1:

Section 1 Definitions
"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by either party, including personal data of end customers.
"Business Day" means a day other than Saturday, Sunday, or a public holiday in India.
"Fees" means the amounts set out in Schedule B.

Page 2:

Section 2 Purpose
The Vendor shall provide software services to the Customer in India.

Section 3 Payment
The Customer shall pay Fees within 15 days of invoice. Late payments attract a penalty of 5% per week.

Page 5:

Section 5 Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days' notice.

Section 5.1 Early Termination
If the Customer terminates before the Initial Term ends, the Customer shall pay an early termination fee equal to 12 months of Fees.

Page 10:

Section 7 Confidentiality
The Receiving Party shall protect Confidential Information as defined in Section 1, except as provided in Schedule B.

Section 8 Renewal
This Agreement shall automatically renew for another 12 months unless 90 days' notice is provided.

Page 12:

Section 9 Liability
The Vendor's liability is capped at ₹10 lakh, except for indemnity claims.

Section 10 Indemnity
The Customer shall indemnify and hold harmless the Vendor against all claims arising from use of the services, including third-party IP claims.

Page 20:

Section 11 Intellectual Property
The Customer hereby assigns all right, title and interest in any intellectual property created using the services, including user-created work, on a perpetual basis.

Section 12 Non-Compete
The Customer shall not compete with the Vendor in India for 24 months after termination.

Section 13 Data
The Vendor may collect, share and retain personal data of the Customer's users for 10 years.

Section 14 Dispute Resolution
All disputes shall be finally settled by arbitration in Mumbai under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

Section 15 Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.

Page 50:

Section 16 Notice Override
Notwithstanding Section 5, termination by Party A shall require 90 days' notice.

Page 80:

Schedule B — Fees and Exit
Schedule B modifies the termination provisions.
Liability shall be unlimited for any breach of Confidential Information.
Notice must be delivered within 5 Business Days including Saturday.
Fees are non-refundable.

Page 90:

Annexure A — Security
As set forth in Annexure A, the Vendor will use commercially reasonable security.

Amendment No. 2 dated 15 March 2026
Pursuant to Amendment No. 2, Section 5 is hereby amended to read as follows: termination by the Customer shall require 90 days' notice and remains subject to the early termination fee in Section 5.1.
This Amendment is between the Vendor and the Customer. It does not automatically supersede Schedule B.
